Formgiving Book

Catalogue accompanying the Formgiving exhibition at the Danish Architecture Center in Copenhagen. The publication explores BIG’s ideas on how architecture can respond to the challenges shaping our planet.

Year: 2020
Studio: BIG
Press: designboom, TASCHEN, art4d

Mitgera 11

Identity and sales dossier for Mitgera 11, a twenty-two-home development in Tarragona’s Part Baixa. The name, Catalan for party wall, sets the tone for the wordmark and for a boxed ring binder covering the location, the building, the apartment types and the specifications.

Year: 2026
Context: Llavor Homes

Park Residences

Sales dossier for two timber buildings by Proforma Studio in an apricot orchard in Goght, Armenia. The architects’ drawings are redrawn as one set, from the site to each apartment.

Year: 2026
Context: Proforma Studio

Formgiving Exhibition

Graphic design for BIG’s largest exhibition to date at the Danish Architecture Center in Copenhagen, including posters, flyers, and maps. The exhibition explored the evolution of life from the Big Bang to possible futures through a chronological journey.

Year: 2019
Studio: BIG
Press: designboom, DAC, Scandinavian Design

Pallet, Tyre, Iroko

Publication for a dwelling module in Makeni, Sierra Leone, built from pallets, used tyres and iroko. The construction drawings are redrawn alongside the site, climate and assembly sequence.

Year: 2016
Context: Master’s thesis, San Pablo CEU

3daysofdesign Graphic Identity

Graphic identity for 3daysofdesign 2024, developed at BIG around the theme “Dare to Dream”. The project explored the possibilities of design through an optimistic visual language centred on imagination, inspiration, and the belief that anything is possible.

Year: 2024
Studio: BIG
Press: Dezeen, IFDM, Interior Daily

BHQ Signage & Wayfinding

An iconography and signage system for BIG’s headquarters in Copenhagen, from the floor directory and door signs to the pictograms on them. One grid, one line weight: every icon belongs to the same family.

Year: 2022
Studio: BIG

From the Arctic Edge to Ocean Bloom

Editorial design and custom 3D illustrations for the scientific publication documenting the Arctic Edge to Ocean Bloom ocean experiment, translating complex research and experimental setups into accessible visual storytelling.

Year: 2022
Context: Extiv Expeditions

The Architect’s Guide to the Galaxy

Publication developed alongside BIG’s Mars Science City project, exploring the challenges and opportunities of designing architecture on other planets. Rather than a scientific manual, it presents space exploration through an accessible and engaging visual format.

Year: 2018
Studio: BIG

Munsell Color System

An infographic of the Munsell Color System, developed by Albert H. Munsell in the early 20th century, which classifies colour by hue, value and chroma. Modelled in Rhinoceros 3D and refined in Illustrator and InDesign.

Year: 2024

Applied Graphics Office is a design studio founded by Paula Madrid, working at the intersection of space, information and graphic design.

The studio focuses on signage and wayfinding, visual identity and editorial design for studios, institutions and cultural projects, locally and internationally. With a background in architecture, it treats graphic design as part of how things are built and understood: not something applied afterwards, but a layer of how a project is read, navigated and communicated, from the identity system to the finished space.
